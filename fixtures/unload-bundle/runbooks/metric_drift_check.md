# Metric Drift Check

Weekly check for unexpected drift in core metrics.

## Steps

1. Compare this week vs the prior 4 weeks
2. Flag any drift above 5%
3. Open a data-quality ticket
4. Notify the growth team

## Owner

Data platform on-call.
